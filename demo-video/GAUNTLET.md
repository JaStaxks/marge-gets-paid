## Storyboard critic

An independent pass on the storyboard (not the pixels) asked for four changes. What the cut does with them:

- The rail is the repo’s payment path, and the desk is labeled sample data and mock PayPal. The voice says Helen is sample data and that no card is charged. A bridge on the local login reads “Sample board. Not the live booking.”
- The end card keeps both the live URL and the GitHub repo, because that is what the shoot asked for.
- The title keeps the shop’s own line about collecting the deposit. The pay page says the payment is not completed.
- The missed-call stack is type, and the held card says it is a diagram, not a call log.

## What a pixel critic would still notice

- The title, the problem card, and the rail leave open sand. The type is large and anchored, and the sand is the app’s own ground.
- The quote close-up crops the chat so the earlier “Marge lost the model” line is outside the frame.
- The pay-page push crops a little of the phone’s outer edge by the end of that shot. The PayPal and Venmo buttons stay in frame.
- Sample totals on the local board ($141.75 deposits, $175 paid) are the app’s seed data, not money collected in this film.
