# Narration

Voice: edge-tts `en-US-JennyNeural`, rate −4%, then loudness-normalized. No copyrighted music. A self-written felt-piano bed (86 BPM, G major) sits under the voice and fades out on the end card.

## hook
Every missed call costs a plumbing shop the morning. Meet Marge.

## problem
Coastline Heating and Plumbing, San Diego. A water-heater diagnostic in the price book is eighty-nine dollars. The deposit that holds the truck is forty-nine. She books the job before the truck rolls.

## chat_a
This is the live site. Test Customer says the water heater is leaking, and leaves a phone number, an email, and the address.

## chat_b
Marge quotes the price book. She does not invent the numbers.

## chat_c
Then she texts a PayPal link, the same way a dispatcher would.

## pay
The deposit page is the real PayPal checkout. Orders version two created this order, with the job id attached. Venmo is enabled for a US buyer. This film does not complete the payment.

## desk
The owner desk is this same app, running locally. These rows are the sample board. The status chip is AG Grid. A checked webhook can move a job, and a duplicate event does not charge it twice.

## invoice
Helen is sample data, already marked deposit paid. The technician note is priced from the book, and the deposit is credited. The gateway here is the mock. No card is charged.

## rail
Orders version two holds the deposit. The Agent Toolkit creates and sends the invoice. The invoicing REST API is the fallback. Webhooks are checked before they touch the board. Marge collects the deposit while the customer is still in the chat.

## end
Marge Gets Paid. The live desk is marge-gets-paid.vercel.app.
